## The short version

- Honest Learn is free, has no ads, and is for children aged 3 to 18, used through a parent's account.
- You, the parent, are responsible for the account and decide what your children can use.
- Lessons and Hoot (the AI helper) can contain mistakes. They are not a replacement for school, a doctor, or emergency help.
- Please use Honest Learn kindly and fairly, and don't try to break it.
- You can stop at any time and delete everything.

These terms are an agreement between you and [legal name of the organisation] ("we", "us"). Our [privacy notice] explains how we handle information. By creating an account you agree to both.

## Who can use Honest Learn

- You must be a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over to create an account.
- Add only children you are the parent or legal guardian of, or are responsible for with their parent's permission. [Lawyer: decide whether carers, schools or tutors may create accounts.]
- Children aged 3 to 18 may use Honest Learn through your account, with your permission and with the settings you choose.
- One account can have up to 8 children.

## Your account

- Keep your password and parent PIN secret. Children shouldn't know the PIN: it protects settings, sensitive topics, chat history, outside links and extra screen time.
- Use an email address you can check, and verify it. We use it for sign-in codes and important messages about your account.
- You are responsible for what happens on your account. If you think someone else knows your password or PIN, change it straight away.

## What Honest Learn provides

- Lessons, quizzes, brain puzzles, a list of free learning websites and, if you allow it, Hoot, the AI helper.
- Honest Learn is free. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Donations are optional, from the parent area, and don't unlock anything.
- We may add, change or remove features, or stop the service. We will give you reasonable notice of big changes where we can, and you can always download your family's data first.

## Learning content

- Every lesson shows its sources and whether teachers have reviewed it. Many lessons are drafts, written with AI help, that are still waiting for expert review.
- Content may contain mistakes. Please report anything wrong with the report button.
- Health, safety, money and similar lessons give general information for learning. They are **not** medical, legal or financial advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
- Sensitive topics (religion, body and puberty, politics) stay hidden until you unlock them for a child.

## Hoot, the AI helper

- Hoot is a computer program, not a person, teacher or counsellor. It can make mistakes.
- Hoot is designed to give hints, not quiz answers, and not to do homework. Please help your child use it honestly.
- Hoot is **not** a crisis or counselling service. If your child mentions self-harm, suicide or abuse, Hoot tells them to talk to a trusted adult and may name a helpline, and the message is flagged for you. If a child is in danger, call your local emergency number.
- Hoot is off for ages 3–5. You can turn it off for any child. There is a daily message limit.
- When Hoot is on, messages are processed by our AI provider as described in the privacy notice.
- You can read every chat in the parent area. Every reply has a report button.

- try to break, overload, scrape or reverse-engineer the service, or access other families' data;
- share your account with people outside your family, or use Honest Learn to make money (for example, reselling access or content) [lawyer: add exceptions if content is openly licensed].
