# What parents, kids and teachers say about learning apps

Research done in November 2026 using web search. Reddit threads were mostly reached through articles and review sites that quote them, so treat individual quotes as anecdotes. The **patterns** below appeared across many independent sources.

## 1. The biggest complaints, and what Honest Learn does about each

| What people complain about | Where it came up | What Honest Learn does |
|---|---|---|
| **"Free" apps that push paid upgrades at children** (e.g. the 2021 FTC complaint about Prodigy's membership ads aimed at kids) | [EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/technology/popular-interactive-math-game-prodigy-is-target-of-complaint-to-federal-trade-commission/2021/02), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/02/19/prodigy-math-game-ftc-complaint), [Common Sense parent reviews](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/prodigy-kids-math-game/user-reviews/adult) | ✅ Everything is free. There's nothing to buy. The only donate button is inside the PIN-protected parent area, never on a child's screen. |
| **Hard to cancel or leave** (top theme in ABCmouse 1-star reviews) | [Unstar review analysis](https://unstar.app/blog/abcmouse-khan-academy-kids-duolingo-abc-homer-outschool-learning-apps-ranked-2026), [Brighterly](https://brighterly.com/blog/abcmouse-reviews) | ✅ No subscription. Parents can delete the account and every child's data in one tap, or download it all first. |
| **Streak anxiety, chasing points instead of learning** ("1,200-day streak but can't hold a conversation") | [The Decision Lab](https://thedecisionlab.com/insights/consumer-insights/streak-creep-the-perils-of-too-much-gamification), [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-xp-boost-ux-trap-3674572), r/duolingo threads | ✅ No streaks, no leaderboards, no XP. Scores count **first tries** (honest), and finished lessons come back for review on a spaced schedule. A daily time limit ends the session with a "go play" screen. |
| **Kids' video apps drifting into inappropriate content via autoplay and recommendations** | [Good Morning America](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/family/story/parents-inappropriate-content-youtube-54993637), [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/4070678/childproof-youtube-autoplay), [Kinzoo guide](https://kinzoo.com/blog/a-parents-guide-to-youtube-kids) | ✅ No video search and no recommendations. Only reviewer-approved videos, checked with `check-videos.mjs`, play one at a time in the privacy-enhanced player. No autoplay, and leaving the player is blocked. |
| **Aggressive sales, guilt-tripping parents, loans they didn't understand** (Byju's in India) | [Context / Thomson Reuters Foundation](https://www.context.news/money-power-people/loss-after-loss-indian-parents-say-byjus-pushed-them-into-debt), [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1039797/worst-decision-of-my-life-byjus-accused-of-driving-parents-into-debt-with-predatory-practices), [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/parents-fume-as-ed-tech-platforms-force-them-to-buy-online-courses/67795.html) | ✅ No sales team, no calls, no paid tiers. We collect the parent's email only for verification and password reset, and never use it for marketing. |
| **"Free IQ test" sites that hide the result behind a paywall, inflate scores, or claim more than they can measure** | [RIOT IQ articles](https://www.riotiq.com/articles/online-iq-tests/are-online-iq-tests-scams) (a vendor, so biased), [Trustpilot reviews](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/aptilink.io?page=5) | ✅ Our reasoning puzzles are free, show every result, **never give an IQ number**, and say plainly that only a qualified psychologist can measure IQ. |
| **AI doing students' homework hurts learning** (study of 3.2M math problems on ALEKS) | [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/homework-artificial-intelligence-cheating/) | ✅ Hoot gives hints only; quiz answers stay on the server until earned. Hoot's replies are checked for answer leaks before a child sees them. |

## 2. What parents praise

- **Free, no ads, no subscriptions** is the most-praised feature of Khan Academy Kids ([Common Sense](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/khan-academy-kids), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/1378467217), [Pastory review](https://pastory.app/articles/khan-academy-review/)). ✅ This is our model too.
- **Privacy:** parent accounts with child profiles ([Common Sense privacy rating](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/khan-academy-kids)). ✅ Same design: nicknames and birth years only.
- **Seeing progress:** some parents want clearer progress tracking ([Modulo review](https://www.modulo.app/all-resources/khan-app-kids)). ✅ The parent area shows lessons done, quiz scores and minutes. **This week** totals were added after this research.
- **Gentle, creative activities for little ones.** ✅ Read-aloud, emoji-rich lessons and a "parent tip" in every lesson for ages 3–8.

## 3. Still to do because of this research

- ⬜ **Offline mode:** families with patchy internet (common in India and many other countries) need lessons to work offline.
- ⬜ **More languages.** Every successful kids' app in India and elsewhere offers local languages.
- ⬜ **Weekly email summary for parents** (opt-in, no marketing).
- ⬜ **Run the app past real families** (5–10 parents, a mix of ages) before launch, and read their feedback the same way.
