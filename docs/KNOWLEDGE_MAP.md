# Honest Learn — Knowledge Map (v0.1.0)

> **Status:** draft — every outcome needs review by two subject reviewers before publishing

**5 age bands · 11 subjects · 51 cells · 220 learning outcomes**

This is the minimum knowledge every learner should have at each age, before college. It is **universal** (not tied to one country) and is the brief for content writers and reviewers.

**Aligned to:**
- UNESCO Global Proficiency Framework (reading & mathematics, grades 1–9) — minimum proficiency levels
- Cross-checked against Cambridge Primary/Lower Secondary, US Common Core + NGSS, India NCF 2023 (coverage check only; no text copied)

## Age bands

| Band | Ages | Reading | Session | How the AI helps | Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Little Explorers** | 3–5 | pre-reader | 10 min | read-aloud and simple questions only; no free chat | pictures, sound, play, parent beside the child |
| **Early Learners** | 6–8 | early reader | 15 min | 'Ask why' helper, short safe answers, parent can see every chat | concrete, hands-on, short videos (<5 min), praise effort |
| **Discoverers** | 9–11 | independent reader | 20 min | hint-ladder tutor (nudge → bigger hint → similar worked example); never gives homework answers | small projects, experiments, asking why |
| **Thinkers** | 12–14 | fluent reader | 25 min | hint-ladder tutor; Socratic questioning | debate, abstract ideas, choice of topics, respectful (not babyish) tone |
| **Builders** | 15–18 | fluent reader | 30 min | Socratic tutor + study planner; never writes essays or solves assignments | exam-ready, real-world, careers, independence |

## Sensitive topics (parent PIN required)

Outcomes marked 🔒 are hidden until a parent unlocks that topic for their child with their PIN.

- **🔒 religion** — Beliefs, religious practices and festivals. Taught descriptively (what people believe), never as advocacy. Hidden unless a parent unlocks it with their PIN.
- **🔒 body** — Puberty, reproduction, relationships and consent education. Hidden unless a parent unlocks it with their PIN.
- **🔒 politics** — Government systems, elections, contested history and current issues. Taught with multiple perspectives and sources. Hidden unless a parent unlocks it with their PIN.
